# High-Ticket Executive Coaching: Client Acquisition System Blueprint

This system is engineered for elite Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), authority building, and friction reduction. It is specifically designed for coaches targeting the C-Suite, VP-level leaders, and scaling founders.

---

## 0. STRATEGIC FOUNDATION (THE PSYCHOLOGY)

Before implementing the wireframes, align all communication with these core psychological pillars:

### Key Psychological Triggers
- **Authority & Status:** Maintain a "sought-after" expert image; avoid the "needy" salesperson vibe.
- **Time Leverage:** Focus on the elimination of manual, low-ROI tasks (manual outreach, content grind).
- **Institutional Trust:** Build a professional "infrastructure" that resonates with C-Suite expectations.
- **Predictability:** Shift from "hope-based" marketing to a data-driven inbound engine.
- **Selective Exclusivity:** Filter for high-intent, high-budget corporate clients only.

### Core Pain Points Addressed
- **The Content Hamster Wheel:** High effort, low conversion content that leads to "vanity metrics" but empty calendars.
- **Brand Erosion:** Low-quality automated outreach that irritates C-Suite leaders.
- **The Discovery Call Drain:** Wasting hours on "pick your brain" sessions or under-qualified prospects.
- **Revenue Volatility:** The feast-or-famine cycle typical of referral-only businesses.
- **The Invisibility Gap:** Being a "best-kept secret" while less-skilled coaches capture market share.

## 2. LEAD CAPTURE & CONVERSION LOGIC

### Form Architecture
To maintain high conversion rates while ensuring only $5k–$25k leads get through, use a two-step "Micro-Commitment" form:

#### Step 1: Basic Contact (Low Friction)
- **Work Email:** (Validate for business domains; block @gmail/@yahoo if possible).
- **First Name:** (For personalization).

#### Step 2: The Executive Qualifier (High Intent - appears after Step 1)
- **What is your primary coaching focus?** (Dropdown: Executive/Leadership, Sales/Growth, Scale-up Operations).
- **What is your current average engagement fee?** (Dropdown: <$2,500, $5,000-$10,000, $10,000-$25,000, $25k+).
- **Primary bottleneck?** (Dropdown: Inconsistent leads, Low-value clients, High manual outreach).
- **LinkedIn Profile URL:** (Crucial for pre-call vetting and building instant rapport).

### Post-Capture Workflow
The 3-step psychological sequence to maximize commitment:

1.  **The Instant Authority Bridge (The Thank You Page):** Immediately after form submission, redirect to a page with a 60-second video. *Copy:* "Your briefing is ready below. But first, here is why 90% of coaches fail at the C-Suite level..." This anchors the importance of the next step.
2.  **The "Pre-Swell" Education (The Briefing):** The core 12-minute briefing delivered via a "Wistia-style" player with a progress bar. Below the video, a dynamic list of "What you're learning" updates as they watch.
3.  **The Logical Progression (The Booking):** Only *after* 8 minutes of the video have passed, a "Book Your Diagnostic Session" button appears. This ensures they have consumed the value and are sold on the mechanism before trying to get on your calendar.

## 3. TECH STACK & OPTIMIZATION BLUEPRINT

### Recommended WordPress Architecture
- **CMS:** WordPress (Self-hosted on WP Engine or SiteGround for performance).
- **Theme:** **GeneratePress (Premium)** or **Hello Elementor**. Zero-bloat foundation is priority.
- **Page Builder:** **Elementor Pro** or **Gutenberg + GenerateBlocks**. "Less is more" design approach.
- **Lead Capture:** **Fluent Forms** or **Typeform**. Fluent Forms preferred for speed.
- **Video Hosting:** **Wistia** or **Vimeo Business**. (Never use YouTube for B2B authority funnels).

### Performance Optimization Requirements
- **Target Load Time:** Under 1.5 seconds on desktop, under 2.5 seconds on mobile.
- **Asset Management:**
    - **Caching:** WP Rocket or FlyingPress.
    - **Image Optimization:** ShortPixel (WebP format).
    - **CDN:** Cloudflare for global edge caching.
    - **Script Control:** Perfmatters to disable unused scripts.
- **Mobile Responsiveness:** Prioritize "Mobile-First" for the busy executive.

### Tracking, Event Triggers & Analytics
- **GTM (Google Tag Manager):** Centralized tracking hub.
- **Conversion API (CAPI):** Essential for Meta/LinkedIn tracking.
- **Event Triggers:**
    - `Lead_Step1`: Fired upon email/name submission.
    - `Lead_Qualified`: Fired upon completion of Step 2 Qualifier.
    - `Video_Watch_50%`, `Video_Watch_75%`, `Video_Complete`: Measure briefing consumption.
    - `CTA_Click_Booking`: Fired upon clicking the booking button.
- **Behavioral Analytics:** **Hotjar** or **Microsoft Clarity**.
- **Attribution:** Precise UTM parameter tracking for all traffic sources.
