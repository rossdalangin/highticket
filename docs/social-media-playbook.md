# Executive Social Media Playbook

High-ticket authority is built through **Calculated Insight**, not daily volume. Use these frameworks to drive traffic from LinkedIn and private executive communities.

## 1. The "Institutional Pivot" Post
**Objective:** Identify leaders currently navigating change.
- **Hook:** "Most {company} leaders fail the '90-Day Scale Test' because they rely on Referral-Only trust."
- **Body:** Contrast the "Hamster Wheel" (manual content/outreach) with the "Beacon Strategy" (intent-based inbound).
- **CTA:** "I've mapped this architecture in a 12-minute briefing for VPs. Link in first comment."

## 2. The "ROI Audit" Post
**Objective:** Drive downloads to the Lead Magnet.
- **Hook:** "We just audited 50 mid-market leadership teams. The same 4 revenue leaks appeared in 80% of them."
- **Body:** Bullet point the leaks (e.g., Retention Gap, Decision-Maker Invisibility).
- **CTA:** "Download the full Leadership ROI Audit framework at [YourLink]/audit."

## 3. The "C-Suite Logic" Post
**Objective:** Pre-sell the $25k engagement.
- **Hook:** "Why my $25k engagements start with a 'No'."
- **Body:** Explain your qualification protocol. You only work with organizations where the ROI is at least 10x. This builds massive exclusivity.
- **CTA:** "Book a Diagnostic Session to see if your retention revenue math aligns: [YourLink]/diagnostic"

## 4. Community Engagement (Slack/Discord)
When a Founder asks for advice on scaling:
- **Response:** "I've seen this exact bottleneck at [SimilarOrg]. I actually put together a Board-level summary on how we solved it using an Intent Beacon. Happy to share the link if it helps."
- **Why it works:** It’s helpful, not salesy, and provides an 'Institutional Asset'.
