# High-Ticket Growth Hacks & Traffic Strategies

Deploying the infrastructure is Phase 1. Driving high-intent C-Suite traffic is Phase 2. Use these elite growth hacks to fuel your acquisition engine.

## 1. The "LinkedIn Intent Beacon" Hack
Don't just post content. Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator to track "Job Changes" at the VP and C-Suite level.
- **The Play:** When a new VP is hired, they have 90 days to prove impact. Send them a personalized DTR link: `yoursite.com/briefing?company={company}&name=[Name]`.
- **Why it works:** You are solving their immediate "New Role" anxiety with an architectural solution.

## 2. The "Institutional PDF" Podcasting Strategy
When guesting on B2B podcasts, don't just give a general CTA.
- **The Play:** "I've put together a specific Leadership Retention Audit for [Podcast Name] listeners. You can grab the framework at `yoursite.com/audit`."
- **Setup:** Create a page using the **Lead Magnet / Asset Capture** template.

## 3. The "Board-Level" Referral Loop
Instead of asking for general referrals, ask for "Board Introductions."
- **The Play:** Provide your current clients with your **ROI Case Study** link. "If you know another Founder navigating this scale, this 2-minute Case Study shows the math we used here."

## 4. Dark Social "Personalization"
Share your Executive Briefing link in private Slack/Discord communities for Founders.
- **The Play:** Use the DTR parameters to make the link preview and landing page feel like a 1:1 message.
