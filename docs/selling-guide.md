# The Consultant’s Playbook: Selling "Executive Acquisition"
## How to Pitch, Price, and Close $10k+ Infrastructure Engagements.

This guide is for agencies and consultants using the **Executive Acquisition** system to deliver high-value results for their clients.

---

### 1. The Value-Based Pitch
Stop selling "WordPress Themes." Start selling **"Institutional Client Acquisition Infrastructure."**

**The Core Argument:**
"Your coaching is world-class, but your digital infrastructure signals 'individual freelancer' rather than 'institutional partner.' We bridge that gap by deploying a predictive inbound engine that identifies, qualifies, and pre-sells C-Suite decision-makers before you even meet them."

### 2. The Pricing Model
Do not charge per hour. Charge for the **Result.**

- **Tier 1: Infrastructure Setup ($2,500 - $5,000)**
  - Full theme deployment via One-Click Generator.
  - Basic branding and copy integration.
  - Setup of 3 core funnels (Briefing, Lead Magnet, ROI Library).

- **Tier 2: The Inbound Engine ($7,500 - $12,500)**
  - All Tier 1 assets.
  - Custom copywriting for the Executive Briefing (VSL).
  - Setup of Dynamic Text Replacement (DTR) for top 50 target accounts.
  - Integration with their CRM and Email Nurture sequences.

- **Tier 3: The "Boardroom" Partner ($20,000+ / engagement)**
  - Full acquisition strategy.
  - Ghost-writing of 3 high-authority Resource Library whitepapers.
  - Ongoing LinkedIn Outreach management using the provided scripts.
  - Monthly ROI and Intent Mapping reports.

### 3. The Discovery Call Script (High-Ticket Focus)

**Phase 1: The "Agitation" Question**
"I see you're doing quite a bit of content on LinkedIn. Are those likes translating into actual $15k+ corporate contracts, or are you just getting 'peer praise' while your calendar remains inconsistent?"

**Phase 2: The "Mechanism" Reveal**
"The problem isn't your expertise; it's your infrastructure. Most executive prospects 'vibe-check' you before replying. If they see a generic B2C site, they bounce. We deploy an **Institutional Intent Method** that uses identity resolution and authority bridging to prove your ROI in 12 minutes."

**Phase 3: The "One-Click" Assurance**
"We handle the entire tech-stack. We have a proprietary infrastructure generator that builds your entire funnel in seconds. We focus on the strategy; the tech is already codified."

### 4. Handling Common Consultant Objections

- **"I can do this in Elementor myself."**
  - *Response:* "You could spend 40 hours building the layouts, but you'd still be missing the pre-coded DTR logic, the specific VSL time-delay triggers, and the 1.5s performance optimization that we've already engineered. Our system is a proven conversion framework, not just a layout."

- **"It's too expensive."**
  - *Response:* "This system is designed to land $10k–$25k contracts. If it lands just *one* engagement that you otherwise would have missed due to a lack of institutional trust, the system has paid for itself 2x over."
