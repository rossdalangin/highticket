# The Diagnostic Session: Turning Leads into Engagements

Once a C-Suite lead has consumed your Executive Briefing and booked a Diagnostic Session, your goal is to transition them into a high-ticket ($5k-$25k) engagement.

## 1. The Pre-Call Vetting
Before the call, use the LinkedIn URL provided in their intake form to:
- Identify recent institutional pivots or financial milestones.
- Map their professional network for common connections to anchor trust.

## 2. The Session Structure (45 Minutes)
- **00-05: The Frame.** Establish yourself as the architect, not a salesperson. "My goal today is to map your specific leadership bottlenecks against our Institutional Method. If there's a fit, we'll discuss implementation."
- **05-20: Deep Diagnosis.** Ask: "What is the specific cost of your current [Pain Point] over the next 12 months?" Let them quantify the ROI for you.
- **20-35: The Gap Map.** Demonstrate how the "Institutional Intent Beacon" and "Authority Infrastructure" close the gap they just identified.
- **35-45: Engagement Options.** Present two clear tiers (e.g., $10k Intensive vs. $25k Transformation).

## 3. High-Ticket Objection Handling
- **"It's too expensive."** Response: "Compared to the $2.4M in retention revenue we mapped earlier, this is a 1% infrastructure investment. Does the math not align on your end?"
- **"We need to talk to the Board."** Response: "I've included a Board-level ROI summary in your Resource Library. Would you like me to join that 15-minute briefing?"
