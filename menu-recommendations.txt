# EXECUTIVE ACQUISITION: MENU ARCHITECTURE RECOMMENDATIONS

For a high-ticket coaching practice, your menu should minimize friction and anchor institutional trust.

## 1. PRIMARY NAVIGATION (Header)
The goal is to provide proof and insights without distracting from the main funnel.

- **HOME:** The core high-conversion landing page.
- **ROI CASE STUDY:** A deep dive into institutional results (use the ROI Case Study template).
- **ASSET LIBRARY:** High-value whitepapers and frameworks (use the Asset Library template).
- **BLOG / INSIGHTS:** Strategy pieces that build authority.
- **[CTA BUTTON]:** "GET STARTED" or "BOOK DIAGNOSTIC" - Link directly to the #cta anchor on the home page.

## 2. COMPLIANCE & RECOVERY (Footer)
The footer is where you handle administration and secondary recovery.

- **PRIVACY POLICY:** Essential for institutional trust and compliance (GDPR/CCPA).
- **TERMS OF SERVICE:** Outlines the diagnostic session expectations.
- **ROI DIAGNOSTIC:** Link to the booking page/anchor.
- **SUPPORT / CONTACT:** Provide an executive-level contact email.

## 3. HIDDEN FUNNEL PAGES (Not in Menus)
These pages should NOT be in your main navigation as they require previous context:

- **THANK YOU / AUTHORITY BRIDGE:** The page shown immediately after form submission.
- **EXECUTIVE BRIEFING:** The VSL page containing the 12-minute ROI briefing.
